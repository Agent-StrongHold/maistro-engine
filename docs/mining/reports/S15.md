**Slice**: S15 (stronghold a2a + prompts vs. engine equivalents)  
**Status**: In Progress

## Summary

Analyzing Stronghold's a2a (delegate, lifecycle, guest_peers) and prompts (store, diff, routes) against maistro-engine targets. **Known lead confirmed**: engine dropped both `user_id` AND `tenant_id` from delegation audit trail in guest_peers, and also stripped tenant scoping entirely. Additional gaps found in state validation, metadata handling, and prompt scoping philosophy (org vs. user).

## Notes

- **Audit trail thinning confirmed**: Known lead verified. Engine dropped both `user_id` (product-agnostic) and `tenant_id` (Stronghold-owned) from guest_peers delegation audit. Product-agnostic `user_id` should be re-added.
- **Tenant scoping removed**: guest_peers registry no longer scoped by tenant; per ADR-019/068, Stronghold owns tenancy decisions but engine should at least document the scope boundary.
- **Prompt scoping philosophy diverges**: Stronghold uses org_id, engine uses user_id. This is an architectural choice (multi-org vs. per-user) that needs explicit spec/ADR.
- **Delegation budget missing**: Engine has A2ABroker with DelegationBudget, circular guards, and depth limits that Stronghold lacks. Should be ported or validated against ADR-058/SPEC-182.
- **State machine validation added**: Engine added explicit TaskStatus transition validation that Stronghold lacks — security/correctness improvement.
